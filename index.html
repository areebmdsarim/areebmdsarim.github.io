About me

I am a researcher at NIT Warangal, advised by Prof. Sumit Mishra and Prof. Chanchal Suman. My work covers multilingual and low-resource NLP and parallel algorithms for optimization. More broadly, I am interested in machine learning and language-model systems.

I received my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from IIIT Guwahati in 2024. Before joining NIT Warangal, I worked as a software engineer at Cogneo Technologies, building LLM-based features and backend services. Earlier internships were at Black Basil Technologies (retrieval-augmented generation) and Tekizma (distributed machine learning).

Publications

mReDDIT
FIRE 2026
Multilingual Regret Detection and Domain Identification in English, Kannada and Tulu

Areeb Md Sarim, Aliya Khan, Siddharth Singh, Chanchal Suman, Sumit Mishra

Working Notes of FIRE 2026, mReDDIT Shared Task. Under review. code

A system built on XLM-RoBERTa-large with a class-weighted loss and minority-class oversampling, plus a retrieval step for Kannada. It placed 1st in Kannada, 2nd in Tulu and 4th in English, with macro-F1 of 0.9439, 0.7109 and 0.7964.

Parallel WFG
EMO 2027
Multicore Parallel WFG for Hypervolume Computation

Sumit Mishra, Jasteg Singh, Anji Babu, Carlos A. Coello Coello, Areeb Md Sarim

EMO 2027, International Conference on Evolutionary Multi-Criterion Optimization. Submitted. code

A Rust and Rayon implementation of the WFG hypervolume algorithm with an O(n log n) parallel running time under the PRAM-CREW model. It is up to 6.85× faster than the serial version, cutting 130.05 s to 18.99 s on 40 points in 15 objectives.
